We teach the ‘objective reality’ of technical laws and class struggle, ensuring children comprehend the ‘chain of development’ independent of their spontaneous feelings. This is political education, not trade-union tinkering.

They master the 'constructional equipment' not as operators but as citizens who decide its use.
