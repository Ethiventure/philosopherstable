## QUESTION

Automation and robotics have replaced almost all human necessary work.
How does this change the education system? What do we teach children?

## PASS 1 — Lenin

The question assumes a vacuum where labour has vanished. In Petrograd,
the machine does not free the child; it merely strips away the 'trade
union' excuse for ignorance. The worker's consciousness is not
spontaneous; it must be brought from without. [Calls for an integral
philosophy of production; education as vanguard organization.]

[...'plastic powers', 'temporary skeletal forms', 'automatic
